Five Reasons to Forget About Golden Hour

July 20, 2026

Golden hour gets a lot of hype, and some of it is deserved. Here are five reasons to forget about it.

1. It's unreliable.

If you rely on golden hour, your productivity will go in the toilet.

2. The light changes rapidly

Trying to constantly adjust your exposure can lead to excess cortisol

3. Shadows can take on a life of their own

Shadow can add to the composition but can mess with dynamic range and ruin your chi.

4. Color temperature drift

Auto white balance struggles here and you can waste a lot of time and take years off your life trying to keep up with it.

5. Do your really want to get up that early?

Depending on the season, it can ruin your sleep, your mood, cause brain fog, compromise your immune system, and you'll probably catch a cold or the flu.
