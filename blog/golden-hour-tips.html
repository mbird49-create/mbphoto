Five Things I Look For at Golden Hour

July 20, 2026

Golden hour gets a lot of hype, and most of it is deserved. Here are five things I actually pay attention to on location.

1. Direction of light

Backlighting versus side-lighting completely changes the mood of a shot.

2. How fast the light is changing

The last 10 minutes before sunset move fast — plan shots in advance.

3. Where the shadows fall

Long shadows can be a compositional tool, not just an obstacle.

4. Color temperature drift

Auto white balance struggles here; I usually lock it manually.

5. The five minutes after sunset

Some of my favorite frames come from the afterglow, not the peak.
